## Overview

Lumina is a wellness startup focused on mindful living. I created a complete brand identity that communicates calm, clarity, and modern sophistication.

## Deliverables

- Logo and wordmark variations
- Color palette and typography system
- Business cards and letterhead
- Social media templates
- Brand guidelines document

## Design Direction

Soft gradients, organic shapes, and a serene color palette of sage green and warm neutrals define the Lumina brand.
