## Overview

A comprehensive redesign of a fashion e-commerce platform focused on improving conversion rates and mobile usability. The project involved user research, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping.

## Challenge

The existing platform had a high cart abandonment rate and poor mobile experience. Users struggled with navigation and the checkout process felt cluttered.

## Solution

- Simplified navigation with clear category hierarchy
- Streamlined 3-step checkout flow
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Bold typography and product photography focus

## Results

The redesign led to a 35% increase in mobile conversions and significantly improved user satisfaction scores in post-launch surveys.
