## Overview

Studio Nova needed a website that matched their bold creative identity. I designed and developed a fast, animation-rich site that showcases their portfolio and services.

## Approach

- Performance-first with Astro's static generation
- Custom scroll animations and page transitions
- CMS integration for easy content updates
- Fully responsive across all devices

## Key Pages

- Hero with animated typography
- Case study grid with hover effects
- Team page with personality
- Contact form with Formspree integration

## Outcome

The site loads in under 1 second and has helped the agency win several new client pitches.
